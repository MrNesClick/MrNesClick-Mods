# MCNesclick

![MCNesclick](../assets/mcnesclick/logo.png){ align=right width="128" }

A collection of small, vanilla-friendly survival additions: the kind of things you didn't know you were missing until
you try them. Everything is tuned from a single config file. For Fabric, NeoForge and Forge, from Minecraft 1.18 to
26.3.

![A lit campfire keeps the camp free of monsters](../assets/mcnesclick/campfire-comparison.png)

## In short

- **[Cooked apple](apples.md#cooked-apple)**: cook an apple in a furnace, smoker or on a campfire. 8 hunger and 12
  saturation instead of 4 and 2.4.
- **[Emerald and diamond apples](apples.md#emerald-and-diamond-apples)**: crafted one from the other, from a golden
  apple. Hero of the Village and Luck before trading, Haste II and Night Vision before mining.
- **[Apples from leaves](apples.md#apples-from-leaves)**: choose how often oak and dark oak leaves drop apples, and let
  every other kind of leaves drop them too.
- **[Campfire protection](campfire-protection.md)**: a lit campfire stops hostile mobs from spawning naturally around
  it, 16 blocks around and 8 above and below by default.
- **[Configurable](config.md)**: each feature can be tuned or turned off, and every recipe can be replaced by a data
  pack, KubeJS or CraftTweaker.

## Requirements

Needed on **both the client and the server**: it adds items. Download the file that matches your loader and Minecraft
version.

| Loader | Minecraft versions |
|---|---|
| Fabric (**Fabric API required**) | every version from 1.18 to 26.3 |
| NeoForge | 1.20.4, 1.20.6, 1.21, 1.21.1, 1.21.3 to 1.21.11, 26.1, 26.1.1, 26.1.2, 26.2, 26.3 |
| Forge | 1.18.2, 1.19.2, 1.20.1, 1.21.1, 1.21.3 to 1.21.11, 26.1, 26.1.1, 26.1.2, 26.2, 26.3 |
