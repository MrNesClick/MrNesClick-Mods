## Bugs and suggestions

Found a bug or have an idea? [Open an issue](https://github.com/MrNesClick/MrNesClick-Mods/issues/new/choose) and pick
the form for your mod. Search the [existing issues](https://github.com/MrNesClick/MrNesClick-Mods/issues) first, and for
a crash, upload `logs/latest.log` or the crash report to [mclo.gs](https://mclo.gs). English or French are welcome.
